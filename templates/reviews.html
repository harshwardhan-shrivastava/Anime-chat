How Reviews & XP Work

Rating

Rate anime on 5 stars. Rate episodes on 5 stars too (shown as /10 on the card). Your rating + optional comment becomes a review.

Pick stars, write your thoughts, and hit Post Review. That's it — your opinion is now part of the community.

XP & Votes

Every like earns the author XP, every dislike hurts it — it's all about your ratio. Posting reviews also gives a small XP bonus. Your XP bar fills as you climb toward the next rank.

10 likes + 0 dislikes = strong ratio. 5 likes + 5 dislikes = 50% ratio. Keep your reviews helpful and constructive to earn more likes!

Ranks

S+ 15,000+ XP
S 5,000 XP
A 2,000 XP
B 1,000 XP
C 500 XP
D 0 XP (everyone starts here)

Higher ranked reviews show first in the feed. Climb the ranks to get your reviews seen by more people!
