How Reviews & XP Work

Ratings & Grades

Rate anime and episodes on 10 stars. An anime's headline badge (S+/S/A/B/C/D) is earned by C-rank and above reviewers, weighted by rank and by their XP — a high-XP S+ voice outweighs a fresh B. A separate audience score shows everyone's average, so a downvote mob can't hijack the badge.

Hidden Gems

An anime the crowd loves (8+/10 average) that no trusted reviewer has graded yet shows as a Hidden Gem — not falsely crowned, not buried. One more reason for C+ reviewers to weigh in.

Trusted XP (the tiebreaker)

Every anime's badge shows its Trusted XP — the combined XP of the reviewers shaping its grade. Two anime with the same 10/10 aren't equal: the one backed by more Trusted XP wins Vs-battle ties. Trusted XP also ranks the anime itself:

S+ 5,000+ XP
S 2,000 XP
A 1,500 XP
B 1,000 XP
C 500 XP
? below 500 XP — ungraded

Rank Up & Earn XP

New users get an XP boost on their first reviews to climb faster. Every like earns XP; every dislike costs it. Higher rank = your reviews weigh more toward an anime's badge. Replying to reviews requires C rank (500 XP) — so brand-new D-rank accounts can't jump into a comment section to kill the vibe. Spam to attack and you lose the very reputation that gives you weight.

Review XP vs Your XP

Each review earns its own Review XP, shown as a D–S+ badge on the card. Every vote is worth points that scale with the voter's rank — a D like is +10, an S+ like is +150 (see the Vote Points box above). That's the review's clout — separate from your profile XP (the bar by your name, which tracks your rank and reviewing power).

Dislikes Need a Reason

To stop vote-bombing, a dislike requires a reason — one per person, per review. That reason is public and 👍/👎-rateable by everyone. Your dislike only counts if the community agrees with your reason (more 👍 than 👎). Give a bad or brigading reason and it backfires: your dislike does nothing and shows as Contested. Review-bombing is nearly impossible when every bomber has to post and defend a reason.
