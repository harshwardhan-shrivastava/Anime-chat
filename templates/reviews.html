How Reviews & XP Work

Ratings & Grades

Rate anime on 5 stars. Each rating maps to a grade — 5★ = S, 4★ = A, 3★ = B, 2★ = C, 1★ = D. An anime's headline badge (S+/S/A/B/C/D) is earned only from B+ reviewers, weighted by rank. A separate audience score shows everyone's average — so a downvote mob can't hijack the badge.

Rank Up & Earn XP

New users get an XP boost on their first reviews to climb faster. Every like earns XP; every dislike costs it. Higher rank = your reviews weigh more toward an anime's badge. Spam to attack and you lose the very reputation that gives you weight.
