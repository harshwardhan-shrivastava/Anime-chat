How Reviews & XP Work

Ratings & Grades

Rate anime and episodes on 10 stars. An anime's headline badge (S+/S/A/B/C/D) is earned by C-rank and above reviewers, weighted by rank and by their XP — a high-XP S+ voice outweighs a fresh B. A separate audience score shows everyone's average, so a downvote mob can't hijack the badge.

Hidden Gems

An anime the crowd loves (8+/10 average) that no trusted reviewer has graded yet shows as a Hidden Gem — not falsely crowned, not buried. One more reason for C+ reviewers to weigh in.

Trusted XP (the tiebreaker)

Every anime's badge shows its Trusted XP — the combined XP of the reviewers shaping its grade. Two anime with the same 10/10 aren't equal: the one backed by more Trusted XP wins Vs-battle ties. Trusted XP also ranks the anime — and every review's Review XP uses the exact same ladder:

S+ 5,000 XP
S 3,000 XP
A 2,000 XP
B 1,500 XP
C 1,000 XP
D 500 XP
? below 500 XP — ungraded

Rank Up & Earn XP

New users get an XP boost on their first reviews to climb faster. Every like earns XP. Higher rank = your reviews weigh more toward an anime's badge. Disliking and replying to reviews require C rank (500 XP) — D-rank accounts can only like, so brand-new accounts can't jump into a comment section to kill the vibe. Spam to attack and you lose the very reputation that gives you weight.

Review XP vs Your XP

Each review earns its own Review XP, shown as a D–S+ badge on the card. Every like is +10 and every dislike is −5 — flat, no rank multipliers. That's the review's clout, earned purely from the points it attracted — separate from your profile XP (the bar by your name, which tracks your rank and reviewing power, since by the time you're C+ your likes and dislikes actually count).

Dislikes & Replies

Dislikes require C rank (500 XP) — one click, no reason needed, just like a like. D-rank accounts can only like, so brand-new accounts can't jump in and bomb a review — by the time someone is C rank they've earned real standing in the community. Review-bombing is dead on arrival.

Reply War

War is for replies — C+ reviewers post one take (Positive 👍 or Negative 👎) on any review, and a 24-hour battle begins. Anyone votes 👍/👎 on every take; the entry with the best like-ratio wins (100👍 / 50👎 still loses to 75👍 / 0👎). The winner's reply is the only one shown under the review, tagged Positive or Negative — and a Negative winner knocks the review's XP, so a bad take can genuinely be beaten. Open War in the nav for every battle, hottest first.
