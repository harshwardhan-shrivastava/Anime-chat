September 1 · Rating Power v2

The badge now works both ways — fair for every verdict

The elixir system, rank-priced and mirrored
  • One vote = the voter's rank power: D +3, C +5, B +7, A +9, S +11, S+ +15 on a like; dislikes drain C −2 up to S+ −7.
  • Green (6–10) likes pour into the anime/episode tank — RED (1–4) is the mirror: a like takes the same rank value out, a dislike hands it back. An S+ like on a 1/10 Trash review costs the title 15 XP, exactly what an S+ like on a 10/10 earns.
  • Anime/episode tanks and every review's own badge keep their sign — mobbed red verdicts sink the tank into negative XP (red "Mobbed down" state).
  • Anti-bombing gates: D-rank can only like green/neutral reviews (+3); dislikes and RED-review likes require C rank (500 XP); F accounts can't vote at all.
  • Rating word badges (TRASH → ABSOLUTE CINEMA) moved next to the Review XP box and made bigger on every card.
September 1 · Rating Power v1

The 10-Star Verdict system

Every rating is a verdict, every vote is earned
  • Ratings out of 10 with word verdicts: 1 Trash · 2 Awful · 3 Meh · 4 Hopeless · 5 Mid · 6 Good enough · 7 Good · 8 Great · 9 Peak · 10 Absolute Cinema.
  • Reviews earn their own D–S+ Review XP badge on every card; the anime's Overall XP tank = the sum of all its reviews.
  • Like/dislike ratio keeps reviewers honest — dislike-ratio abusers drop to F rank and lose their vote, so the ladder can't be farmed by hating.
